How Apollo Technology handles information when you use its Instagram account filtering and export features.

Effective date: October 12, 2026 · 生效日期：2026年10月12日

1. Overview

Apollo Technology (“the Extension,” “we,” “us”) is an independently developed browser extension that helps users filter, review, organize and export information made available on Instagram pages accessible to them. This Privacy Policy describes the categories of information involved in the Extension’s operation, the purposes for which that information is processed, and the choices available to users.

2. Information Processed

Depending on the Instagram page, available information and the features you choose to use, the Extension may access or process:

  • Account information visible on the pages you access, such as usernames, profile names, profile links, account descriptions, verification status and publicly displayed account metrics.
  • Follower or following list information displayed to your browser, and filter criteria such as geographic references, account metrics and other supported account attributes.
  • Settings and preferences that you enter into the Extension to perform filtering and export operations.
  • Information you choose to export into a file on your device.

Not all attributes are available for every account. The Extension does not require you to provide your Instagram password directly to Apollo Technology.

3. Purposes of Processing

The Extension processes information to provide user-requested functionality, including displaying and filtering account information, applying selected criteria, organizing results, and preparing data exports. Processing is limited to functions presented in the Extension and initiated or configured by the user.

4. Browser Permissions

The Extension may request access to Instagram pages and browser capabilities needed for its features. Such access can be used to read information displayed on supported pages, store extension settings and initiate user-requested downloads. Chrome displays the permissions requested by the installed version of the Extension. The Extension is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Instagram or Meta.

5. Storage, Exports and Retention

When you use an export feature, the resulting file is downloaded to a location controlled by your browser or operating system. You are responsible for how you store, retain, share or delete files that you export. Settings or temporary processing data may be stored using browser storage and can generally be removed by clearing extension data or uninstalling the Extension, subject to browser behavior.

Information you view or export may also be subject to Instagram’s own policies. This page does not govern Instagram’s processing or information you independently share with other services.

6. Data Sharing and Commercial Use

Apollo Technology does not sell Instagram account data to data brokers or advertisers. Information processed through the Extension is intended to support the functionality requested by the user, not to create advertising profiles. Any optional external service or account synchronization feature, if made available in a future version, must be accompanied by appropriate disclosure and an update to this Policy before use.

7. Security

We seek to limit access to information to what is reasonably necessary for the Extension’s functions. No browser extension or computing environment can be guaranteed entirely secure. Users should protect their browser sessions, exported files and devices, and should use the Extension only where they have an appropriate right to access and process the information involved.

8. Your Choices

  • You can stop using the Extension or remove it through Chrome’s extension management interface.
  • You can delete exported files from your device and clear extension-related browser data through your browser settings.
  • You can contact us about this Policy or privacy questions using the address below.

9. Children's Privacy

The Extension is not designed for children under 13 and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Extension’s functionality, data practices or applicable requirements change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

1. 概述

Apollo Technology（以下简称“本插件”或“我们”）是一款独立开发的浏览器扩展程序，用于帮助用户筛选、查看、整理与导出其有权访问的 Instagram 页面中提供的账号信息。本隐私政策说明插件运行涉及的信息类型、处理目的以及用户可作出的选择。

2. 处理的信息

根据用户访问的页面、可获得的信息以及使用的功能，插件可能访问或处理以下信息：

  • 页面展示的账号信息，例如用户名、个人资料名称、资料链接、账号简介、认证状态及公开显示的账号指标。
  • 浏览器页面展示的粉丝或关注列表，以及地区相关信息、账号指标及其他受支持的筛选条件。
  • 用户在插件中输入的筛选设置与使用偏好。
  • 用户主动选择导出到其设备中的数据。

并非所有账号均提供上述信息。用户无需直接向 Apollo Technology 提供 Instagram 登录密码。

3. 信息处理目的

插件处理信息用于实现用户主动选择的功能，包括查看和筛选账号、应用筛选条件、整理结果以及生成导出文件。处理范围以插件向用户提供、由用户启动或配置的功能为限。

4. 浏览器权限

插件可能请求访问 Instagram 页面及实现功能所需的浏览器权限，以读取支持页面中显示的信息、保存插件设置或执行用户发起的下载操作。实际请求的权限以已安装版本在 Chrome 中显示的权限为准。本插件与 Instagram 或 Meta 不存在官方隶属、授权或背书关系。

5. 存储、导出与保留

用户使用导出功能时，结果文件会下载到由浏览器或操作系统控制的位置。用户负责管理、保留、共享或删除其导出的文件。插件设置及临时处理数据可能保存于浏览器存储中，通常可通过清除相关浏览器数据或卸载插件进行移除，具体取决于浏览器行为。

用户查看或导出的信息也可能受到 Instagram 自身政策的约束。本政策不适用于 Instagram 自身的数据处理，也不适用于用户自行向其他服务分享的信息。

6. 数据共享与商业用途

Apollo Technology 不会将 Instagram 账号数据出售给数据经纪商或广告商。插件处理信息的目的是实现用户请求的功能，而不是建立广告画像。如果未来版本提供可选的外部服务或账号同步功能，我们将在使用前进行适当披露并更新本政策。

7. 安全措施

我们致力于将信息访问限制在实现插件功能所合理需要的范围内。任何浏览器扩展程序或计算环境都无法保证绝对安全。用户应妥善保护浏览器登录会话、导出文件和设备，并仅在具有适当访问及处理权限的情况下使用插件。

8. 用户选择

  • 用户可以随时停止使用插件，或通过 Chrome 扩展程序管理界面将其卸载。
  • 用户可以删除设备中的导出文件，并通过浏览器设置清除与插件相关的浏览器数据。
  • 如对本政策或隐私事项有疑问，可通过以下邮箱联系我们。
